I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at ENS de Lyon on the ANR CORJOLIS project.

I hold a PhD in Economics from the RITM (Université Paris-Saclay), under the supervision of Matthieu Crozet, defended in July 2026. During my PhD, I visited the University of Bristol.

My research lies in spatial economics, labor economics and economic history. I am particularly interested in spatial and occupational worker relocation, and in spatial polarization. I enjoy working with large datasets (BTS, RP, DV3F) and apply a range of methods, including Dynamic Discrete Choice Models, Quantitative Spatial Economics, and Spatial Econometrics.
